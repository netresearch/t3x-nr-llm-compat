- mfd/ai-filemetadata 1.7.1 and later with a `ModifyRequestDataEvent` listener: while the AI File Metadata integration is active, a listener that changes anything but the system prompt (for example adds a web-search tool) makes the bridge refuse every request, so no alt texts are generated: an upload logs the refusal as a warning, and `ai:generate-alt-texts` stops at the first file. An empty image or an empty answer also stops `ai:generate-alt-texts` at that file, as the original 1.7.1 does. Once the integration is active, `nrllm:compat:status` names the registered listeners. Remove or adapt such a listener, or leave the integration disabled ([#96](https://github.com/netresearch/t3x-nr-llm-compat/issues/96)).
